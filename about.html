About Minority Doctors in North Carolina

Connecting communities with minority healthcare professionals while inspiring future generations to pursue careers in medicine.

Our Story

MedMinority was founded by high school student Niya Barry after her family experienced how difficult it could be to find a Black physician in North Carolina. That experience revealed a broader challenge: minority physicians are not always easy for patients and families to discover.

For some patients, connecting with a physician who shares or understands their cultural background can contribute to a greater sense of comfort, trust, and understanding. Representation also matters beyond the examination room, as young students benefit from seeing healthcare professionals whose journeys make careers in medicine feel possible.

MedMinority was created to support both needs. The platform is building an opt-in directory that helps people discover minority physicians while also providing educational resources and highlighting pathways into healthcare for future professionals.

Our Mission

To increase access to minority healthcare professionals, promote healthcare equity and inspire future generations to pursue careers in medicine

Our Vision

A future where patients can more easily discover healthcare professionals and every child can see themselves represented in medicine.

Why Diversity Matters in Healthcare

Diversity in healthcare improves communication, builds trust, reduces disparities and creates more culturally responsive care.

Patients often report greater satisfaction and improved healthcare outcomes when physicians understand their cultural experiences and perspectives.

Increasing diversity within medicine also strengthens healthcare systems by bringing varied experiences, ideas and approaches to patient care.
