Pathway to a Medical Career

A straightforward overview for students beginning to explore medicine.

High School

Build strong foundations in science, mathematics, writing and communication while exploring healthcare careers.

Undergraduate Study

Complete a bachelor's degree and the prerequisite coursework required by the medical schools you plan to apply to.

Medical School

Study medical sciences, develop clinical skills and complete supervised clinical rotations.

Residency

Complete supervised postgraduate training in a chosen specialty.
