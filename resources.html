Career Roadmap

The Path to Becoming a Doctor

Every journey is different, but these are the major stages students generally follow when preparing for a career as a physician.

1

High School

Build a strong foundation in biology, chemistry, mathematics and communication skills.

2

Undergraduate Study

Complete a bachelor's degree and the required science courses for medical school admission.

3

Medical School

Study medical sciences, complete clinical rotations and develop practical patient-care skills.

4

Residency

Receive supervised professional training in a chosen medical specialty.

5

Medical Practice

Become licensed, continue professional learning and begin serving patients and communities.
