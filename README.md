# MedMinority 🩺

**MedMinority** is a web-based healthcare directory designed to help residents across North Carolina discover and connect with minority doctors and healthcare professionals. By bridging technology, community outreach, and healthcare equity, MedMinority aims to increase representation in medicine and improve access to culturally competent care.

---

## 🌟 Mission & Overview

Finding a healthcare provider who understands your cultural background, language, or lived experiences can significantly improve patient outcomes and trust in medicine. **MedMinority** provides an intuitive platform where community members can locate trusted providers, access educational resources, and support diversity in healthcare.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Doctor Directory & Search:** Easily browse and search for minority healthcare providers in North Carolina.
* **Featured Provider Cards:** Highlighted medical professionals showcasing their specialties and clinical backgrounds.
* **Healthcare Equity & Resources:** Informational guides on why representation matters, alongside guidance for aspiring healthcare students.
* **Community Engagement & Mentorship:** Pathways for students to find mentors and for community members to recommend doctors or volunteer.
* **Volunteer & Partner Forms:** Interactive ways for individuals and organizations to get involved and support the mission.
