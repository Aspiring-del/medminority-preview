North Carolina Physician Directory

The directory is being built through voluntary provider submissions and manual review.

Directory Development

Provider Profiles Are Being Added

MedMinority is currently collecting information directly from physicians who choose to participate. Profiles will be published after the submitted professional information and participation consent have been reviewed.

Provider Opt-In Form

Search by Specialty

Profiles will identify each physician's listed specialty so visitors can narrow the directory to the type of care they are seeking.

Search by Location

Practice city and region information will help visitors discover physicians serving communities across North Carolina.

Confirm Through Official Sources

Directory profiles will point visitors toward official health-system or practice information whenever available.

What Will Appear in a Profile?

Each published profile is intended to include a physician's name, specialty, practice location and an appropriate official source link.

The directory will not display private contact details, patient information or unsupported claims about care quality.

Independent Educational Project

MedMinority does not provide medical advice, recommend individual physicians or verify quality of care. Visitors should confirm current information through official health-system, practice and licensing sources.
