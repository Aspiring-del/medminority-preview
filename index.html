Why Representation Matters

Representation in healthcare can help strengthen communication, trust and cultural understanding between patients and physicians.

Our mission is to increase the visibility of minority healthcare professionals and strengthen healthcare access for diverse communities.

How It Works

Building the Directory Carefully

MedMinority is beginning with African American physicians in North Carolina and can expand to additional minority physician communities as the project develops.

Physician Opt-In

Physicians voluntarily submit their professional information and consent to being considered for the directory.

Information Review

Submitted details are reviewed against public professional sources before a profile is added.

Official Sources

Visitors are directed to official health-system, practice or licensing sources for current information.
